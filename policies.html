Privacy

  • EditSutra edits your videos on your own computer. Your footage, audio and captions are not uploaded.
  • Activating a licence sends your licence key and an anonymous computer ID once, to tie the licence to that computer.
  • Help improve EditSutra (your choice). The panel asks you once (Yes / No, thanks). Only if you say yes, EditSutra shares, as text, how its tools were used and what you changed by hand afterwards: the settings you chose, what each tool changed on your timeline (cuts, B-roll, sounds, transitions, zooms, effects), the changes you then made yourself (for example a cut you brought back, B-roll you added or removed, a caption word you corrected), the times of these changes, effect and transition names, and a few spoken words near each change. It is sent at most once a day, together with your licence key (or, during the trial, a code for your computer). We use it only to make EditSutra's automatic edits better. Your videos, audio, images, and file, clip, folder, project and sequence names are never sent. You can turn sharing off at any time at the bottom of the panel ("Sharing: on/off"); nothing is kept or sent after that. To see or delete what you shared, or for any privacy complaint, email rishavskillverse@gmail.com and we act within 30 days.
  • When you buy, the payment page collects your name, email and payment details to process the order and email your licence key. We use your email only for your licence, support and important product updates.
