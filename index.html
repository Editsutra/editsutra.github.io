Natural silence cutting

Long pauses become a short breath, short ones stay: about 8 cuts a minute, never choppy. Every cut lands inside the silence, not on a word.

AI retake picker, offline

Keeps the last good take and removes false starts, repeated lines and talk to the crew. Runs on your computer, every cut checked before it is made.

Modes, preview & undo

Medium for full videos, Tight for Shorts, Light keeps natural pauses. Preview or mute instead of cutting, and one-click Undo.

Editable captions

Hindi, English and Hinglish on an editable caption track. Translate to English. Caption only an In/Out range.

Voice cleanup

Removes background noise, echo and hum on your computer. The original track is kept, muted.

65 built-in sound effects

Placed automatically by meaning: whooshes on cuts, ka-ching on money talk, a shutter on “photo”. Preview, add with one click, or use your own SFX folder.

Jump cuts that disappear

Matches the eye level across each cut so the jump vanishes, zooms only when the move is too big, and leads the sound with J/L cuts. Each one on or off.

B-roll from your library

Your own clips matched to what is being said, placed muted above your footage.

Vertical Shorts

Questions

What is EditSutra?

A plugin panel for Adobe Premiere Pro that automates repetitive parts of talking-head editing, pauses and retakes, captions, voice cleanup, sound design, Shorts and more, and leaves the results editable on your timeline.

Which Premiere Pro versions are supported?

Premiere Pro 2024 or newer. The beta was tested on Premiere Pro 2026.

Does it support Hindi and Hinglish captions?

Yes, Hindi, Hinglish and English, with Hinglish spelled the way creators usually write it. Automatic captions can still contain mistakes, so review them before publishing.

Does EditSutra work offline?

Editing does. You need internet once to download the AI files on first open, once to activate your key, and for the media-download tool.

What does the 7-day trial include?

Every feature in the current beta, on your own projects, for 7 days from the first time you open EditSutra.

Do I need a credit or debit card?

No. The trial needs no card and no account.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is charged. The tools lock until you enter a licence key; your projects are untouched.

Is it a subscription?

Pick what suits you. Monthly (₹129) renews each month and you can cancel future renewals any time. Standard Annual (₹1,499) renews yearly at the shown price. The Early Bird offer (₹999 for 12 months) does not renew automatically unless you choose to.

What does a licence include?

Every included EditSutra feature for one individual editor, for as long as your plan is active.

How many computers can I activate?

One licence works on one computer.

Can I transfer my licence to another computer?

Yes, if you replace or change your computer, contact support and we move your licence to the new one.

Are updates included?

Yes. Every update released while your plan is active is included, and the app shows when one is ready.

What do I need for the Pro AI Auto-Editor?

Your own Claude Pro (or higher) subscription from Anthropic, which is paid separately and not included. The Pro plan gives you the editing skill pack, the editing styles and the WhatsApp community. The EditSutra plugin itself (Beta plan) needs no Claude subscription.

What if a feature doesn't work on my footage?

That's what the free trial is for, test your own footage first. During the beta, send us a screenshot and the log file from the app so we can fix it.

How do I contact support?

Email rishavskillverse@gmail.com.

What are the refund and cancellation terms?

There is nothing to cancel: it is not a subscription. All sales are final, so please use the free 7-day trial to test EditSutra on your own footage before buying. If your key does not arrive or will not activate, email support and we fix it.
