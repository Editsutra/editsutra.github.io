Questions editors ask

What is EditSutra?

A panel for Adobe Premiere Pro that does the slow parts of editing (cuts, captions, graphics, sound, Shorts and more) and leaves everything editable on your timeline.

Does it support Hindi and Hinglish captions?

Yes: Roman Hinglish, Devanagari Hindi and English, each with its own speech model. Review captions before publishing, or paste your script for exact wording.

How do the captions get into Premiere?

EditSutra writes a standard SRT file and adds it to your sequence as a Premiere caption track, so you can edit the words and style them in Premiere. Animated word-by-word captions come in as a see-through video on their own track.

What do I need?

Premiere Pro 2024 or newer. A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), or Windows 10/11 64-bit (beta). 8 GB memory (16 GB recommended) and about 4 GB of disk for the AI files.

Can it download videos?

Yes. Paste a YouTube, Instagram or Pinterest link and the clip lands on your timeline at the quality you pick (highest, 1080p, 720p or MP3). Use it for your own videos or footage you have the rights to.

Does it work offline?

Editing does. You need internet once to download the AI files, once to activate your key, and for the media-download tool.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The 7-day trial needs no card and no account. When it ends nothing is charged; the tools lock until you enter a licence key.

Is it a subscription?

Your choice. Monthly (₹129) renews each month; cancel future renewals any time. Standard Annual (₹1,499) renews yearly. Early Bird (₹999 for 12 months) does not renew unless you choose to.

How many computers can I use?

One licence works on one computer. Changing computers? Contact support and we move it.

Are updates included?

Yes, every update and new tool released while your plan is active. The plugin installs bug fixes itself.
