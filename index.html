Tight, Medium & Light modes

Medium for full videos, Tight for Shorts, Light keeps natural pauses. Manual settings too.

Editable captions

Hindi, English and Hinglish on an editable caption track. Translate to English. Caption only an In/Out range.

Voice cleanup

Removes background noise, echo and hum on your computer. The original track is kept, muted.

Smooth cuts & transitions

B-roll from your library

Questions

What is EditSutra?

A plugin panel for Adobe Premiere Pro that automates repetitive parts of talking-head editing — pauses and retakes, captions, voice cleanup, sound design, Shorts and more — and leaves the results editable on your timeline.

Which Premiere Pro versions are supported?

Premiere Pro 2024 or newer. The beta was tested on Premiere Pro 2026.

Does it support Hindi and Hinglish captions?

Yes — Hindi, Hinglish and English, with Hinglish spelled the way creators usually write it. Automatic captions can still contain mistakes, so review them before publishing.

Does EditSutra work offline?

Editing does. You need internet once to download the AI files on first open, once to activate your key, and for the media-download tool.

What does the 7-day trial include?

Every feature in the current beta, on your own projects, for 7 days from the first time you open EditSutra.

Do I need a credit or debit card?

No. The trial needs no card and no account.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is charged. The tools lock until you enter a licence key; your projects are untouched.

Is the purchase recurring?

No. It is a one-time payment of ₹999 during the beta.

What does the one-time licence include?

A licence for one computer, kept for good, with every future update and version included at no extra cost.

How many computers can I activate?

One licence works on one computer.

Can I transfer my licence to another computer?

Yes — if you replace or change your computer, contact support and we move your licence to the new one.

Are future updates included?

Yes — lifetime updates. Every future version is free for licence holders, and the app shows when an update is ready.

What if a feature doesn't work on my footage?

That's what the free trial is for — test your own footage first. During the beta, send us a screenshot and the log file from the app so we can fix it.
