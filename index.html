Questions

What is EditSutra?

A panel for Adobe Premiere Pro that does the repetitive parts of talking-head editing (pauses, retakes, captions, sound, Shorts and more) and leaves everything editable on your timeline.

Does it support Hindi and Hinglish captions?

Yes: Roman Hinglish, Devanagari Hindi and English, each with its own speech model. Automatic captions can still contain mistakes, so review them before publishing, or paste your script for exact wording.

Does it work offline?

Editing does. You need internet once to download the AI files, once to activate your key, and for the media-download tool.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The 7-day trial needs no card and no account. When it ends nothing is charged; the tools lock until you enter a licence key.

Is it a subscription?

Your choice. Monthly (₹129) renews each month; cancel future renewals any time. Standard Annual (₹1,499) renews yearly. Early Bird (₹999 for 12 months) does not renew unless you choose to.

How many computers can I use?

One licence works on one computer. Changing computers? Contact support and we move it.

Are updates included?

Yes, every update released while your plan is active. The plugin shows when one is ready and installs bug fixes itself.

Refunds?

Please test with the free trial first: payments are final. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stop at the end of the paid month. If your key doesn't arrive or won't activate, email support and we fix it.
