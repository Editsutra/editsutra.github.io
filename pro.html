How the skill edits.

The same steps every time, in the order a good editor works. It asks one short question at the plan step and waits for your yes before cutting.

  1. 1

    Set up once

    Checks your computer and installs ffmpeg, the speech model and the rest on Mac or Windows.

  2. 2

    Read the footage

    Length, frame rate, size, rotation and audio tracks of every clip.

  3. 3

    Study the niche

    Measures today's top videos in your niche: cuts per minute, shot length, pace, loudness.

  4. 4

    Transcribe

    Word by word in English, Hindi or Hinglish, fillers and false starts kept as cut signals.

  5. 5

    Plan, then ask

    4-8 sentences: what it will cut, keep and add, and how long the result will be.

  6. 6

    Clean cut

    Every pause, filler, double, retake and slate listed with its reason, flags for you to check.

  7. 7

    Polish

    B-roll, captions on output times, colour, music ducked under voice, loudness.

  8. 8

    Shorts

    Self-contained moments with a hook and a payoff, cut to 9:16.

  9. 9

    Check

    Measures the render and reports numbers. Nothing with a fail is delivered.

Rules it never breaks.

  • Never cuts inside a word; keeps a breath at each cut (about 0.3 s tight, 0.45 s natural)
  • Keeps the last take of anything said twice
  • A 30 ms fade on every cut, so nothing clicks
  • Captions go on last, on the finished timeline's times
  • Your original files are never changed
  • A no-music version is delivered whenever music is added
